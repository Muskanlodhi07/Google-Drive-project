About FileNest

A Better Way to Store Your Files

FileNest is a Google Drive clone designed to provide a seamless and secure cloud storage experience.

The Project

FileNest was created as a personal project to replicate and enhance the functionality of Google Drive. It offers secure file storage, easy sharing options, and a user-friendly interface.

Built with modern web technologies, FileNest aims to provide a seamless cloud storage experience that's both powerful and intuitive.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about FileNest.

Is FileNest free to use?

Yes, FileNest offers a free tier with limited storage. Premium plans are available for users who need additional storage and features.

How secure is my data on FileNest?

FileNest uses end-to-end encryption to ensure your files remain private and secure. Your data is protected both during transfer and while stored on our servers.

Can I access my files offline?

Yes, FileNest allows you to mark files for offline access, making them available even when you don't have an internet connection.

What file types are supported?

FileNest supports all common file types, including documents, images, videos, audio files, and more. There are no restrictions on the types of files you can upload.
